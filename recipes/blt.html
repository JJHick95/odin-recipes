BLT

Description

This recipe will teach you how to build the most delicious BLT sandwich you have ever tasted. The crispy bacon clashes in your mouth with the soft cool tomato and lettuce. The mayo spread on the buttery bread is the cherry on top. Enjoy!

Ingredients

  • Bread
  • Tomato
  • Bacon
  • Lettuce
  • Mayo

Steps

  1. Toast the bread
  2. Spread mayo on the bread
  3. Add crisp lettuce
  4. Add sliced tomato
  5. Add bacon
