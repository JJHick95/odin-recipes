Buffalo Chicken

Description

This recipe will teach you the ins and outs of making a delicious buffalo chicken meal. This buffalo chicken meal is served best during lunch or dinner! Take it up a notch with different heat levels to test your tolerance. Cool down the spice with either ranch or blue cheese! Enjoy!

Ingredients

  • Buffalo Sauce
  • Garlic
  • Honey
  • Onion Powder
  • Lime Juice
  • Chicken

Steps

  1. Make your buffalo sauce
  2. Preheat oven and prepare chicken
  3. Bake
  4. Pick a side item
