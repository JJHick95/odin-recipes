Waffles

Description

This recipe will teach you how to make the best plate of waffles for breakfast at any time of the day. The powdered sugar and fruit topping will compliment the all the flavors in such a way that will make you crave them day and night. Enjoy!

Ingredients

  • Waffle Mix
  • Eggs
  • Vanilla
  • Cinnamon
  • Milk

Steps

  1. Add waffle mix to a bowl
  2. Add cinnamon to the bowl of waffle mix
  3. Crack some eggs and add them to the bowl
  4. Add a splash of vanilla
  5. Mix the ingredients in the bowl until smooth
  6. Pour onto a waffle maker and bake for 5 minutes
